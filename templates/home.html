Why we built this

Grocery prices in Nepal vary wildly — and most people don't realise it

The same litre of milk can cost NPR 80 at one store and NPR 110 at another, just streets apart. A bag of rice can vary by 30% depending on the brand and where you buy it.

Most apps built for this problem are designed for Western supermarkets. We built Micro-Savings specifically for Kathmandu's market — the stores people actually shop at, the brands people actually buy, and prices that reflect reality.

  • Prices pulled from real Kathmandu stores — Bhatbhateni, Salesways, General Market
  • Recommendations always exclude the brand you already bought (no pointless same-brand suggestions)
  • Healthier alternatives flagged for food items — not for soap or shampoo
  • Prices update automatically when you log receipts — no manual maintenance needed
